# Audit rubric

Score each rule pass (✓), partial (~) or fail (✗). Quote the course or
lesson that proves it. The total is the number of passes out of 10.

| # | Rule | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start-here module | First course is open to everyone, explains how the community works and gives a task for today | No such module, or it is locked, or it is a long welcome video with no task |
| 2 | First win in week 1 | A task doable in under 20 min within the first 3 lessons, ending in something to post | First task appears later than day 7 of the path, or there is none |
| 3 | Result before day 14 | The path reaches a result the member can name within two weeks | Two weeks of theory before any result |
| 4 | Short lessons | Main-path lessons are 3–7 minutes (text lessons: one screen) | Most main-path lessons over 10 minutes |
| 5 | One task per lesson | ≥ 80 % of main-path lessons end in exactly one task | Under 50 %, or lessons with several tasks |
| 6 | Visible verbs | Tasks start with publish, write, upload, record, send… | "Reflect", "think about", "consider" |
| 7 | Result-named modules | Titles say what is achieved, 2–5 words | "Module 1", "Introduction", "Week 3" with nothing else |
| 8 | Deliverable per module | Each module ends in something to show in the feed | Modules that end without an output |
| 9 | Unlock by days | Main path opens by days since joining; levels only for bonuses | Essentials locked behind a level |
| 10 | Next step in sight | The last module points to the next challenge, phase, tier or role | The path just ends |

Partial = the rule holds for part of the path; say which part.

## Reading the evidence

- Video length: from the lesson's video metadata when present. Unknown →
  score rule 4 on text length and say that video length was not available.
- "Main path" = what a member who follows the default order meets in the
  first 60 days. Libraries of replays and bonus courses are scored only on
  rules 7 and 9.
- A task counts when the lesson text states one action the member does and
  can show. "Watch the next video" is not a task.

## Verdict guide

- `keep` — on the path to the promised result and passes rules 4–6.
- `fix` — right content, wrong packaging: title, missing task, wrong unlock,
  two ideas in one lesson (split).
- `merge` — two lessons teach the same step.
- `re-record` — over 10 minutes on the main path, or no action, or it
  promises something the community no longer offers.
- `remove` — duplicate, outdated tool or price, or not on the path to the
  promised result. Removing is the creator's action.
