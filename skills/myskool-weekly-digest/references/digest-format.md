# Weekly digest — structure and examples

Everything published is **plain text** (no `**`, `#`, backticks, no
@mentions). Short lines, simple emoji as bullets, one link per item. Write in
the community's language. Target ≤ 250 words.

## Structure (drop any section that has nothing real in it)

```
<Title: emoji + "lo mejor de la semana" + dates>

<1 line: the week in one sentence with one number>

🔥 Lo más comentado
- <first name>: <what the thread is about, ≤ 15 words> → <link>  (×1–3)

🏆 Victorias
- <first name>: "<their words>" → <link>  (×1–3)

💡 Por si te lo perdiste (respuestas que valen oro)
- <question in members' words> → <link>  (×1–2)

🙋 ¿Quién ayuda?
- <first name> pregunta <topic> → <link>  (×1–2)

📅 Esta semana
- <day, time, timezone>: <event> → <link>  (only from list_events, config or the creator)

👋 Bienvenidos a los <N> nuevos: <up to 5 first names>. Preséntate aquí → <link>

<closing question members can answer in the comments>
```

## Title formulas

- ES: `🗞️ Lo mejor de la semana · 22–28 sep`
- ES: `🗞️ Resumen semanal: 6 victorias, 3 hilos que no te puedes perder`
- EN: `🗞️ This week in <group> · Sep 22–28`
- EN: `🗞️ Weekly recap: 6 wins and 3 threads worth your time`

## Engagement prompts (D2–D4)

Build each from a real struggle, with evidence ("7 members asked about…").
Formulas:

- **Share your situation**: "¿En qué paso estás con <struggle>? Cuéntalo en
  una línea y te digo el siguiente." / "Where are you stuck with <struggle>?
  One line, and we'll point you to the next step."
- **Small public commitment**: "Esta semana: <tiny action>. Comenta 'hecho'
  cuando lo hagas." / "This week's 10-minute challenge: <tiny action>."
- **Before/after**: "Muestra tu <thing> de antes y de ahora 👇"
- **This or that**: "¿<option A> o <option B> para <goal>? ¿Por qué?"

Each prompt: title ≤ 70 chars, body 2–4 lines, one question, no links unless
it points to a lesson from config/knowledge base.
