# Weekly patterns by engine

One anchor post per day. Adapt days to the creator's real call days; keep
the wins post and the what's-next post every week.

## Challenges

| Day | Anchor | Category |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Challenge kick-off + daily check-in thread | challenge / check-in |
| Tue | Tip of the day (one obstacle, one fix) | tips |
| Wed | Featured win (a real member, with permission) | wins |
| Thu | Flash challenge (1 day) when the feed is quiet | challenge |
| Fri | Streak ranking | challenge |
| Sat/Sun | Closing call + announce next week | announcements |

## Live calls

| Day | Anchor | Category |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Agenda of the week + open the case queue | announcements |
| Tue | Call 1 (audit / hot seat) | — event |
| Wed | Clip or written takeaway from call 1 | lessons / replays |
| Thu | Call 2 (topic of the month) | — event |
| Fri | Before and after of one case | wins |
| Sat | Round-up + what's next | announcements |

## Program

| Day | Anchor | Category |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Module or practice of the week | deliveries |
| Tue | Worked example | tips |
| Wed | Open question | questions |
| Thu | Review call | — event |
| Fri | Wins | wins |
| Sun | Reminder for whoever fell behind + what's next | announcements |

## Post shapes

- **Kick-off**: what we do this week, today's action with its time, where to
  post the proof.
- **Tip**: one obstacle in members' words, one fix, one question.
- **Wins**: first name, what they did in their words, link. Needs a real
  case: mark `needs her` until there is one.
- **What's next**: the next challenge, phase or call by name and date, and
  the one thing to do before it.

Under 120 words each. One call to action. Plain text.
