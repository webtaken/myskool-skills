# Wins Wall, congratulations and testimonials — formats

Everything published is **plain text** (no `**`, `#`, backticks). Line breaks
and a few simple emoji are fine. Write in the community's language.

## Result types

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| `money/clients` | first client, first sale, raised prices, revenue milestone |
| `launched/shipped` | published the offer, launched the community, shipped the app |
| `tool/skill working` | "Claude Code instalado y mi primer agente corriendo" |
| `habit/consistency` | 30 days posting, finished module 3, first live call |
| `personal milestone` | quit the job, first talk, overcame a fear |
| `helped others` | answered members, shared a template that others used |

## Congratulation comment (≤ 60 words)

Structure: name + what they did (specific) → why it matters (one line) → one
question that invites them to share how.

ES:
```
¡Marta, felicidades! Tu primer cliente de automatización en la semana 3 del programa no es poca cosa 🙌
¿Qué fue lo que más te ayudó a cerrarlo? Seguro a varios aquí les sirve.
— Lucía
```

EN:
```
Huge, Ben! Shipping your first agent while working full time is exactly how this works 🚀
What was the step that finally unlocked it? Others here are stuck right there.
— Lucía
```

## Wins Wall post

Title formulas (pick one, keep the week visible):
- ES: `🏆 Muro de victorias · semana del 22 al 28 de septiembre`
- ES: `🏆 Lo que lograron esta semana (9 victorias)`
- EN: `🏆 Wins Wall · week of Sep 22–28`
- EN: `🏆 This week you shipped, sold and showed up (9 wins)`

Body template:
```
<one line: the week in one sentence, with the count>

🥇 <First name>: "<their words, ≤ 15 words>"
<link>

🎯 <First name>: "<…>"
<link>

… (max 10, strongest first)

<one line that connects the wins to the program: what they have in common>
<one invitation: "¿Tuviste una victoria esta semana? Compártela en los comentarios 👇">
```

ES example:
```
Esta semana 9 de ustedes pasaron de "lo estoy intentando" a "ya funciona" 💪

🥇 Marta: "Cerré mi primer cliente de automatización"
https://www.skool.com/club-agentes-ia/primer-cliente-marta

🛠️ Diego: "Por fin tengo Claude Code corriendo en Windows"
https://www.skool.com/club-agentes-ia/claude-code-windows

📅 Sofía: "30 días seguidos publicando"
https://www.skool.com/club-agentes-ia/30-dias

Lo que tienen en común: hicieron el paso pequeño de esta semana, no el perfecto.
¿Tuviste una victoria? Compártela en los comentarios 👇
```

EN example:
```
9 of you went from "I'm trying" to "it works" this week 💪

🥇 Ben: "Shipped my first agent to a paying client"
https://www.skool.com/agent-builders/first-agent-ben

📅 Priya: "Finished module 3 and booked my first call"
https://www.skool.com/agent-builders/module-3-priya

What they have in common: they did this week's small step, not the perfect one.
Had a win? Drop it in the comments 👇
```

## `.myskool/testimonials.md`

```markdown
# Testimonials · <group name>

## 2026-09-29 · Marta G. · money/clients · income claim
> Cerré mi primer cliente de automatización por 400 USD, gracias al módulo de propuestas.
Link: https://www.skool.com/club-agentes-ia/primer-cliente-marta
Permission: pending
```

- Quote verbatim (you may trim, never reword the meaning or add numbers).
- First name + initial only. No handles, emails or photos.
- `Permission: pending | asked <date> | granted <date> | declined`.
- Tag `income claim` whenever money or earnings are mentioned.

## Permission DM (one per member, approved by number)

ES:
```
Hola Marta! Me encantó tu post sobre tu primer cliente 🙌 ¿Me dejas compartir tu frase (solo tu nombre y la inicial del apellido) en la página del programa? Si prefieres que no, cero problema.
— Lucía
```

EN:
```
Hey Ben! Loved your post about shipping your first agent 🙌 Would you be OK with me sharing your quote (first name and last initial only) on the program page? Totally fine if not.
— Lucía
```
